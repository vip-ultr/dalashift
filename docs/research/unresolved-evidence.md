## Rotation gaps

1. Obtain full metadata and result tables for the IAR/ABU Samaru long-term maize rotation thesis.
2. Ask CDA whether Samaru NGS maize-after-legume results transfer to Tofa's Sudan Savanna soils, rainfall and varieties.
3. Identify local sequence outcomes by crop and season, including yield, fertilizer, labor, residue/fodder use and failed seasons.
4. Establish whether reported “rotation” is truly sequential, or includes intercropping, relay cropping, strip cropping or seasonal crop choice.
5. Seek local evidence on repeated crop cultivation, Striga/Alectra host relationships, and specific disease break effects. No generic break rule is supported yet.
6. Test whether a second crop can mature within the actual remaining rainy season after the first crop. The reviewed regional calendars are not sufficient as a parcel-level feasibility rule.
7. Determine how livestock grazing, residue removal, manure access and burning affect nutrient balances and farmer choices.
8. Any field validation must be designed with CDA and farmers. Literature alone is not field validation.

## Exact agronomist/CDA review requested

- Confirm crop identities and aliases, particularly “late millet,” “beans,” and pepper.
- Confirm that soybean is a separate crop identity, Glycine max, and resolve whether each use of “beans” means soybean, cowpea, common bean or another crop without retroactively changing the interview transcript.
- Confirm whether “white cowpeas” refers to seed colour, a market class or a named variety. The current catalog places the farmer-reported name under cowpea, Vigna unguiculata, while leaving its formal attribute type unverified.
- Provide locally used variety names and maturity classes for the candidate crops.
- Review whether any FAO crop-water range is appropriate as a non-binding context field for Tofa, and specify how to explain it to farmers.
- Review transfer limits of Samaru and Sadoré rotation studies before any engine rule is written.
- Identify local extension recommendations for sowing triggers and crop sequences, with source and date.
- Define which farmer-management inputs must be collected before comparing sequences, especially residue use, irrigation, fertilizer and available labor.

Until these questions are answered, requirements that constrain or rank a crop must remain absent. Farmer-reported sequences may be shown as reports with provenance, but not as literature-backed outcomes.
